First step to participation is the realization of the sufferings that go on around the world. Horrible things happen to innocents. We must give nothing but our best in ending suffering. We cannot tolerate child rape, animal abuse/slavery, predation, wars, diseases, disasters, depression etc.

All of us are equally morally obligated to ending suffering.

Our newly established movement can collaborate in various areas of socio-scientific progress (all of which contribute to achieving our common goal), we've started for example:

  • creating and spreading the proextinction message with social media content
  • translation and linguistic adaptation
  • participation in debates and public forums
  • organising protests and grassroots activism
  • (more scientific and ethical support growing)

We have established our communities in WhatsApp and discord, but we're flexible.

Join these groups and activists will help you get your activism setup. Even teenage activists doing offline and online activism have shown to be capable of volunteering in societal realization towards ending suffering in the world.
