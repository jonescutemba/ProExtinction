What are the evidences of cosmic life ?

If earth can have life the probability is already non zero. That combined with the vastness of universe almost guarantees existence of cosmic life. But that's not it we have more evidences with current primitive technology itself that other planets have life.

Evidence number 1

Organic molecules like nucleobases (the ones that make up dna), amino acids (that make up proteins) and even fatty acids have been found on asteroids. These are that very molecules that make up life. Organic molecules are commonly found everywhere.

The asteroids ryugu, bennu and the murchison meteorite all have been extensively researched. All nucleobases, many protein forming amino acids and carboxylic acids (fatty acids have been found)

The goal is clear ; we should research and determine what is the most vast and thorough extinction possible. Because that's all we can do and like it says it's the only possible effective solution.
